2.1. Purpose
The purpose of this Chapter 1s to establish the mandatory requirements for the preparation,
content, structure, maintenance, and accessibility of the Pharmacovigilance System Master File
(PSMF). It is intended to ensure that the pharmacovigilance system is fully documented in a
manner that supports regulatory oversight, effective risk management, and compliance with
applicable pharmacovigilance obligations.

This Chapter further aims to ensure that the PSMF provides a true, complete, and current
representation of the pharmacovigilance system and enables the Qualified Person for
Pharmacovigilance (QPPV) and the Authority to always verify its adequacy and performance.

2.2. Scope
This Chapter applies to all Marketing Authorization Holders (MAHs) required to maintain a
pharmacovigilance system. It covers all pharmacovigilance systems, including those that are
shared between multiple MAHs or where activities are delegated to third parties.

The provisions of this Chapter apply to the main body of the PSMF and its Annexes, including
all supporting documentation, and shall be complied with in full. MAHs shall ensure that the
PSMF is maintained in a manner that is accurate, complete, up to date, and readily accessible to
the QPPV and the Authority always.

This Chapter 1s aligned with international best practices and makes reference to the Guideline on
Good Pharmacovigilance Practices (GVP) Module II: Pharmacovigilance System Master File
(Revision 2) (EMA/816573/2011/Rev 2).

2.3. Definitions

For this Chapter, the following definitions shall apply:

2.3.1. Audit
A systematic, independent, and documented process for obtaining audit evidence and evaluating it
objectively to determine the extent to which audit criteria are fulfilled.

2.3.2. Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA)
Actions taken to eliminate the cause of an identified non-conformity, deviation, or other undesirable
situation (corrective action), and actions taken to prevent the occurrence of potential non-conformities
(preventive action).

2.3.3. Delegated Activity
Any pharmacovigilance activity or responsibility transferred by a Marketing Authorization Holder
(MAH) to a third party through a documented contractual arrangement, while the MAH retains ultimate
regulatory responsibility.

2.3.4. Deviation
A departure from an approved procedure, process, regulatory requirement, or established
pharmacovigilance system requirement.

2.3.5. Good Pharmacovigilance Practice (GVP)
A set of measures designed to monitor the safety of medicinal products and detect, assess, understand, and
prevent adverse effects or any other medicine-related problems.

2.3.6. Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR)
A structured report containing information describing one or more suspected adverse reactions in an
individual patient.

2.3.7. Marketing Authorization Holder (MAH)
A person or legal entity in whose name a Certificate of Registration has been issued by NAFDAC and
who is responsible for ensuring compliance with all regulatory obligations, including pharmacovigilance
requirements.

2.3.8. Pharmacovigilance
The science and activities relating to the detection, assessment, understanding, and prevention of adverse
effects or any other medicine-related problem.

2.3.9. Pharmacovigilance System
A system used by a Marketing Authorization Holder to fulfil pharmacovigilance responsibilities and
designed to monitor the safety of authorised medicinal products and detect any changes to their benefit-
risk balance.

2.3.10. Pharmacovigilance System Master File (PSMF)
A detailed description of the pharmacovigilance system used by a Marketing Authorization Holder with
respect to one or more authorised medicinal products, which serves as a reference document for the
management, oversight, and inspection of the pharmacovigilance system.

2.3.11. Qualified Person for Pharmacovigilance (QPPV)
A suitably qualified and experienced person appointed by the Marketing Authorization Holder who is
responsible for the establishment, maintenance, oversight, and continuous improvement of the
pharmacovigilance system and for ensuring compliance with applicable pharmacovigilance requirements.

2.3.12. Quality Management System (QMS)
A formalized system comprising organizational structure, responsibilities, procedures, processes, and
resources required to ensure that pharmacovigilance activities are conducted in accordance with
regulatory requirements and quality standards.

2.3.13. Risk Management Plan (RMP)
A detailed description of the risk management system for a medicinal product, including activities and
interventions designed to identify, characterize, prevent, or minimize risks relating to the product.

2.3.14. Periodic Safety Update Report (PSUR) / Periodic Benefit-Risk Evaluation Report (PBRER)
A pharmacovigilance document intended to provide an evaluation of the benefit-risk balance of a
medicinal product at defined time points after authorization.

2.3.15. Safety Database
A validated computerized system used for the collection, storage, processing, management, retrieval, and
reporting of pharmacovigilance data, including Individual Case Safety Reports (ICSRs).

2.3.16. Shared Pharmacovigilance System
A pharmacovigilance system that is used by more than one Marketing Authorization Holder and is
managed under documented agreements defining roles, responsibilities, and oversight arrangements.

2.3.17. Signal
Information arising from one or multiple sources, including observations and experiments, suggesting a
new potentially causal association or a new aspect of a known association between a medicinal product
and an event that warrants further investigation.

2.3.18. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
A written instruction describing the steps to be followed to perform a specific pharmacovigilance activity
consistently and in compliance with regulatory requirements.

2.3.19. Third Party
Any individual, organization, contractor, vendor, distributor, affiliate, or service provider engaged by the
Marketing Authorization Holder to perform pharmacovigilance-related activities.

2.3.20. Validation
The documented process of demonstrating that a computerized system consistently performs according to
its intended purpose and predefined specifications.

2.4. Introduction

2.4.1. The pharmacovigilance system master file provides an overview of the pharmacovigilance
system put in place by the MAH and contributes to the appropriate management of and
improvement(s) to the pharmacovigilance system. The PSMF shall reflect safety information for
medicinal products authorized in Nigeria. The PSMF shall contribute to the planning and
conduct of audits by the MAH, fulfilment of supervisory responsibilities of the QPPV and
inspections by NAFDAC. PSMF provides an overview of the Pharmacovigilance system which
may be requested and assessed by the Agency during marketing authorization application (s) or
post authorization.

2.5. Objectives
2.5.1. PSMF enables the Marketing Authorization Holder and the Qualified Person for
Pharmacovigilance (QPPV) to:

      gain assurance that a pharmacovigilance system has been implemented in accordance with
       the requirements;
      confirm aspects of compliance in relation to the system;
      obtain information about deficiencies in the system, or non-compliance with the
       requirements;
      obtain information about risks or actual failure in the conduct of specific aspects of
       pharmacovigilance.

   2.5.2. This information will contribute to the appropriate management and improvement of
   the PV system.

2.6. Maintenance of the Pharmacovigilance System Master File

2.6.1. Marketing Authorization Holders in Nigeria are required to maintain a pharmacovigilance
system master file and submit to the Agency during application for Certificate of Registration of a
medicinal product, upon request, and upon update. The pharmacovigilance system master file shall
be located either at the site in Nigeria where the Marketing Authorization Holder's main
pharmacovigilance activities are performed, or at the site in Nigeria where the qualified person
responsible for pharmacovigilance operates. Where the PSMF is stored in electronic form, the
location stated must be a site where the data stored can be directly accessed.

2.6.2. Applicants for, and holders of, listings for traditional herbal medicinal products shall also be
required to establish and maintain a Pharmacovigilance System Master File (PSMF).

2.6.3. Marketing Authorization Holders shall continue to ensure that information about QPPV,
name and contact details (phone no and email address), and location of the PSMF are up to date.
2.6.4. When there is a change in the QPPV or location of the PSMF, the MAH shall notify
NAFDAC no later than 30 calendar days.

2.7. Transfer of responsibility for the PSMF
2.7.1. Transfer or delegation of responsibilities and activities concerning the master file shall be
documented (see sections 2.18. and 2.35) and managed to ensure that the Marketing Authorization
Holder fulfils their responsibilities. Since a specific QPPV has responsibility for the
pharmacovigilance system, changes to the pharmacovigilance system master file shall also be
notified to the QPPV in order to support their authority to make improvements to the system. The
types of changes that shall be routinely and promptly notified to the QPPV are:
a. Updates to the PSMF or its location that are notified to the competent authorities.

b. The addition of corrective and/or preventive actions to the pharmacovigilance system master
file (e.g. following audits and inspections). The QPPV shall also be able to access information
about deviations from the processes defined in the quality management system for
pharmacovigilance.

c. Changes to content that fulfil the criteria for appropriate oversight of the pharmacovigilance
system (in terms of capacity, functioning, and compliance);

d. Transfer of significant services for pharmacovigilance to a third party (e.g., outsourcing of
PSUR/PBRER production);
e. Inclusion of products into the pharmacovigilance system for which the QPPV is responsible.

f. Changes for existing products which may require a change or increased workload in relation to
pharmacovigilance activity, e.g., new indications or studies.
g. Changes in arrangements for the provision of the PSMF to competent authorities

2.7.2. Any incoming QPPV must formally accept, in writing, the transfer of responsibility for the
pharmacovigilance system. The QPPV shall also be able to ensure and verify that the
Pharmacovigilance System Master File (PSMF) accurately and consistently reflects the current
structure, processes, and activities of the pharmacovigilance system under his or her responsibility
(see Chapter 1).

2.8. The Representation of Pharmacovigilance Systems
2.8.1. The pharmacovigilance system master file may describe the pharmacovigilance system for
one or more medicinal products of the Marketing Authorization Holder. For different categories
of medicinal products, the Marketing Authorization Holder may, if appropriate, apply separate
pharmacovigilance systems. Each of such systems shall be described in a separate
pharmacovigilance system master file. Those files shall cumulatively cover all medicinal
products of the Marketing Authorization Holder for which a Certificate of Registration has been
issued.
2.8.2. It is anticipated that there may be circumstances in which a single Marketing Authorization
Holder may establish more than one pharmacovigilance system, e.g., specific systems for
particular product types (vaccines, consumer health, etc.), or that the pharmacovigilance system
may include products from more than one marketing authorization holder. In either case, a single
and clearly defined specific system shall be in place to describe each system.

2.8.3. A single QPPV shall be appointed to be responsible for the establishment and maintenance
of the pharmacovigilance system described in the pharmacovigilance system master file.
2.8.4. Where a pharmacovigilance system is shared by multiple Marketing Authorization Holders
(MAHs), each MAH shall ensure that the system adequately covers its registered product(s) and
shall retain full regulatory responsibility. Pharmacovigilance activities may be delegated through
a written agreement; however, accountability shall not be transferred. The MAH’s PSMF may
cross-reference that of the delegated party, provided access is assured to the MAH and the
Agency. The MAH shall ensure oversight and maintain the PSMF in a current and accessible
state.

2.8.5. Where applicable, a list of all pharmacovigilance system master files held by the same
Marketing Authorization Holder should be provided in the annex (see section 2.18); this
includes:
a.     Their location(s),
b.     Details of the responsible QPPV(s) and
c.     The relevant product(s).

2.8.6. The address of the location of the pharmacovigilance system master file should be an
office address which reflects either the site in Nigeria where the main pharmacovigilance
activities of the Marketing Authorization Holder are performed or the site in Nigeria where the
qualified person responsible for pharmacovigilance operates. This address may be different to
that of the Marketing Authorization Holder, for example, a different office of the Marketing
Authorization Holder or when a third party undertakes pharmacovigilance activities.

2.8.7. Similarly, the QPPV details aligned to a product may be those of contract QPPV
responsible for the pharmacovigilance system for a particular medicinal product, and not
necessarily a QPPV directly employed by the Marketing Authorization Holder.

2.8.8. When delegating any activities concerning the pharmacovigilance system and its master
file, the Marketing Authorization Holder retains ultimate responsibility for the
pharmacovigilance system, submission of information about the pharmacovigilance system
master file location, maintenance of the pharmacovigilance system master file, and its provision
to the Agency. Detailed written agreements describing the roles and responsibilities for
pharmacovigilance system master file content, submissions, and management, as well as to
govern the conduct of pharmacovigilance in accordance with the legal requirements, shall be in
place.
2.8.9. When a pharmacovigilance system is shared, it is advised that the partners agree on how to
mutually maintain the relevant sections within their own pharmacovigilance system master files.
Accessibility of the pharmacovigilance system master file to all the applicable Marketing
Authorization Holder(s), and its provision to the Agency shall be defined in written agreements.
Marketing Authorization Holder(s) must have assurance that the pharmacovigilance system used
for their products is appropriate and compliant.

2.9. Content of a Pharmacovigilance System Master File (PSMF)
2.9.1. The pharmacovigilance system master file shall include documents to describe the
pharmacovigilance system. The content of the pharmacovigilance system master file shall have
the headings used in sections 2.10 to 2.18 and shall reflect the global availability of safety
information for medicinal products authorized in Nigeria. The content shall be indexed to allow
for efficient navigation around the document.

2.9.2. The main principle for the structure of the content of the pharmacovigilance system master
file is that the primary topic sections contain information that is fundamental to the description of
pharmacovigilance system. Detailed information is required to fully describe the system, and,
since this may change frequently, it shall be referred to and contained in the Annexes.

b.     A summary curriculum vitae with the key information on the role of the qualified person
responsible for pharmacovigilance;
c.     Contact details;

2.9.5. Where the QPPV is employed by a third party, this shall be clearly indicated, and the name
of the employing organisation shall be provided, irrespective of whether the QPPV’s usual
working address is that of the Marketing Authorization Holder.

2.11. Organizational Structure of the Marketing Authorization Holder
2.11.1. A description of the organizational structure of the Marketing Authorization Holder
relevant to the pharmacovigilance system must be provided. The description shall provide a clear
overview of the company(ies) involved, the main pharmacovigilance departments, and the
relationship(s) between organizations and operational units relevant to the fulfilment of
pharmacovigilance obligations. This shall include third parties. Specifically, the
pharmacovigilance system master file shall describe:

a. The organogram of the Marketing Authorization Holder(s), showing the position of the QPPV
in the organization.

b. The site(s) where the pharmacovigilance functions are undertaken covering individual case
safety report collection, evaluation, safety database case entry, periodic safety update report
production, signal detection and analysis, management of the risk management plan, pre- and

2.12. Delegated activities
2.12.1 The pharmacovigilance master file, where applicable, shall contain a description of the
delegated activities and/or services relating to the fulfillment of pharmacovigilance obligations.
This includes arrangements with other parties in any country, worldwide and if applicable, to the
pharmacovigilance system applied to products authorized in Nigeria.

2.12.2 Links with other organizations, such as co-marketing agreements and contracting of
pharmacovigilance activities, shall be outlined. A description of the location and nature of
contracts and agreements relating to the fulfilment of pharmacovigilance obligations shall be
provided. This may be in the form of a list/table to show the parties involved, the roles
undertaken, and the concerned product(s). The list shall be organized according to service
providers (e.g., medicinal information, auditors, patient support program providers, study data
management, etc.), commercial arrangements (distributors, licensing partners, co-marketing,
etc.), and other technical providers (hosting of computer systems, etc.). Individual contractual
agreements shall be made available at the request of the Agency or during inspection and audit
and the list shall be provided in the Annexes.



2.13. Sources of Data
2.13.1 The description of the main units for safety data collection shall include all parties
responsible on a global basis for solicited and spontaneous case collection for products
authorized in Nigeria. This shall include medicinal information sites as well as affiliate offices
and may take the form of a list describing the country, nature of the activity, and the product(s)
(if the activity is product specific), and providing a contact point (address, telephone, and email)
for the site. The list may be found in the PSMF annexes. The information about third parties
(licensed partners or local distribution/marketing arrangements) shall also be included in the
section describing contracts and agreements. (see sections 2.10 and 2.18)

2.13.2 The description of the main units responsible for safety data collection shall include all
parties involved, on a global basis, in the collection of solicited and spontaneous reports for
products authorised in Nigeria. This shall include medical information services, affiliate offices,
and other relevant entities.

2.13.3 The information may be presented as a list indicating, for each site, the country, nature of

applicable country(ies), product(s) involved, and the primary objective. The list may be included
in Annex C to the PSMF.



2.14 Computerized systems and databases
2.14.1 the location, functionality, and operational responsibility for computerized systems and
databases used to receive, collate, record, and report safety information, as well as an assessment
of their fitness for purpose, shall be described in the pharmacovigilance system master file
(PSMF).

2.14.2 Where multiple computerized systems/databases are used, the applicability of these to
pharmacovigilance activities shall be described in such a way that a clear overview of the extent
of computerization within the pharmacovigilance system can be understood.

The validation status of key aspects of the computer system functionality shall also be described;
the change control, nature of testing, back-up procedures, and electronic data repositories vital to
pharmacovigilance compliance shall be included in the summary, and the nature of the available
documentation shall be described.

2.15 Pharmacovigilance processes
2.15.1 An essential element of any pharmacovigilance system is that there are clear written
procedures in place. Chapter 1 describes the required minimum set of written procedures for
pharmacovigilance. A description of the procedural documentation available (standard operating
procedures, manuals, at global and/or national Level etc.), the nature of the data held (e.g. the
type of case data retained for ICSRs) and an indication of how records are held (e.g. safety
database, paper file at site of receipt) shall be provided in the pharmacovigilance system master
file.

2.15.2 A description of the process, data handling and records for the performance of
pharmacovigilance, covering the following aspects shall be included in the pharmacovigilance
system master file:
a. Continuous monitoring of product benefit-risk profile(s) applied and the result of evaluation
and the decision-making process for taking appropriate measures; this shall include signal
generation, detection and evaluation. This may also include several written procedures and
instructions concerning safety database outputs, interactions with clinical departments etc.;

b. Risk management system(s) and monitoring of the outcome of risk minimization measures;
   several departments may be involved in this area, and interactions shall be defined in written
   procedures or agreements.
c. ICSR collection, collation, follow-up, assessment, and reporting; the procedures applied to
   this area shall clarify what is local and what are global activities.
d. PSUR/PBRER scheduling, production and submission, if applicable (see Chapter 6);
e. Communication of safety concerns to consumers, healthcare professionals and the Agency.
f. Implementation of safety variations to the summary of product characteristics (SmPC) and
   patient information leaflets; procedures cover both internal and external communications.

2.15.3 In each area, the Marketing Authorization Holder (MAH) shall be able to provide
evidence of a system that supports appropriate and timely decision-making and action.

2.15.4 The system description shall be accompanied by a list of processes relating to compliance
management and interfaces with other relevant functions. These interfaces may include, but are
not limited to, the roles and responsibilities of the QPPV, responses to requests for information
from the Agency, literature searching, safety database change control, safety data exchange
agreements, safety data archiving, pharmacovigilance auditing, quality control, and training.

2.16 Pharmacovigilance System Performance
2.16.1 The pharmacovigilance system master file shall contain evidence of the ongoing
monitoring of performance of the pharmacovigilance system including compliance of the main
outputs of pharmacovigilance. The pharmacovigilance system master file shall include a
description of the monitoring methods applied and contain as a minimum:

       a.      An explanation of how the correct reporting of ICSRs is assessed. In the annex,
       figures/graphs shall be provided to show the timeliness of 15-day and 90-day reporting
       over the past year;
       b.      A description of any metrics used to monitor the quality of submissions and
       performance of pharmacovigilance. This shall include information provided by the
       Agency regarding the quality of ICSR reporting, PSUR/PBRERs or other submissions.
       c.      An overview of the timeliness of PSUR/PBRER reporting to the Agency (the
       annex shall reflect the latest figures used by the Marketing Authorization Holder to assess
       compliance);
       d.      An overview of the methods used to ensure timeliness of safety variation
       submissions including the tracking of required safety variations that have been identified
       but not yet been submitted;
       e.      Where applicable, an overview of adherence to risk management plan
       commitments, or other obligations or conditions of Certificate of Registration(s) relevant
       to pharmacovigilance.
2.16.2 Targets for the performance of the pharmacovigilance system shall be described and
explained.

2.16.3 A list of performance indicators must be provided in the Annex to the pharmacovigilance
system master file, alongside the results of (actual) performance measurements.

2.17.1.4 Auditing.
   a. Information about quality assurance auditing of the pharmacovigilance system shall be
      included in the pharmacovigilance system master file. A description of the approach used
      to plan audits of the pharmacovigilance system and the reporting mechanism and timelines
      shall be provided, with a current list of the scheduled and completed audits concerning the
      pharmacovigilance system maintained in the annex referred to in section 2.18. This list
      shall describe the date(s) (of conduct and of report), scope, and completion status of audits
      of service providers, specific pharmacovigilance activities or sites undertaking
      pharmacovigilance, and their operational interfaces relevant to the fulfilment of the
      obligations in the regulations and cover a period of 5 years.
   b. The pharmacovigilance system master file shall also contain a note associated with any
      audit where significant findings are raised. The audit report must be documented within
      the quality system; in the pharmacovigilance system master file it is sufficient to provide a
      brief description of the corrective and/or preventive action(s) associated with the
      significant finding, the date it was identified and the anticipated resolution date(s), with
      cross reference to the audit report and the documented corrective and preventive action
      plan(s). In the annex, in the list of audits conducted, those associated with unresolved notes
      in the pharmacovigilance system master file shall be identified. The note and associated
      corrective and preventive action(s) shall be documented in the pharmacovigilance system
      master file until the corrective and/or preventive action(s) have been fully implemented;
      that is, the note is only removed once corrective action and/or sufficient improvement can
      be demonstrated or has been independently verified. The addition, amendment, or removal
      of the notes must therefore be recorded in the logbook.
   c. The Pharmacovigilance System Master File (PSMF) shall contain a summary note for any
      audit that identifies significant findings. While the complete audit report shall be
      maintained within the Quality Management System, the PSMF shall include a concise
      description of the significant finding(s), the associated corrective and/or preventive
      action(s) (CAPAs), the date the finding was identified, the target completion date(s), and a
      cross-reference to the corresponding audit report and CAPA documentation.
In the Annex containing the audit inventory, audits associated with unresolved significant findings
shall be clearly identified. Such findings and their related CAPAs shall remain documented in the
PSMF until all actions have been fully implemented and their effectiveness has been demonstrated
or independently verified. Any addition, modification, status update, or closure of these notes shall
be documented in the PSMF logbook to ensure appropriate traceability and change control.

2.19 Change control, logbook, versions, and archiving
2.19.1 It is necessary for Marketing Authorization Holders to implement change control systems
and to have robust processes in place to continuously be informed of relevant changes in order to
maintain the pharmacovigilance system master file accordingly. The Agency may request
information about important changes to the pharmacovigilance system, such as, but not limited to:

a.      Changes to the pharmacovigilance safety database(s), which could include a change in the
database itself or associated databases, the validation status of the database as well as information
about transferred or migrated data.

b.     Changes in the provision of significant services for pharmacovigilance, especially major
contractual arrangements concerning the reporting of safety data.
c.     Organizational changes, such as takeovers, mergers, the sites at which pharmacovigilance
is conducted or the delegation/transfer of pharmacovigilance system master file management.

2.19.2 In addition to these changes being documented in the pharmacovigilance system master
file for the purpose of change control (in the logbook), the QPPV shall always be kept informed
of these changes.


2.19.3 Changes to the pharmacovigilance system master file shall be recorded, such that a history
of changes is available (specifying the date and the nature of the change). Changes to the PSMF
must be recorded in the logbook. Descriptive changes to the content of the master file must be
recorded in the logbook.
2.19.4 Change history for the information contained in the annexes may be 'on demand', in which
case the logbook would indicate the date of the revision of PSMF content and/or annex update(s),
the history of changes for annex content would also be updated. Information that is being regularly
updated and is contained in the annexes, such as product and standard operating procedure lists or

maintained outside the PSMF, provided that the change history is preserved and can be made
available to the Agency upon request.

Where the PSMF has not been requested or remains unchanged for an extended period, periodic
review is recommended to ensure accuracy and completeness. Marketing Authorization Holders
shall ensure that the PSMF can be provided to the Agency in a timely manner when requested.

The QPPV shall have permanent access to current and accurate information on the
pharmacovigilance system, including the PSMF and its Annexes, either directly through the PSMF
or through access to the systems used to generate the Annex content.

2.19.6 Marketing Authorization Holders shall be able to justify their approach and have document
control procedures in place to govern the maintenance of the pharmacovigilance system master
file.

shall be kept to ensure that change control is fully implemented.

2.20 Presentation
2.20.1 The pharmacovigilance system master file shall be continuously accessible to the QPPV
and to the Agency. The information shall be succinct, accurate and reflect the current system in
place, which means that whatever format is used, it must be possible to keep the information up to
date and, when necessary, to revise to take account of experience gained, technical and scientific
progress and amendments to the legislative requirements.

 2.20.2 The Pharmacovigilance System Master File (PSMF) shall be continuously accessible to the
QPPV and the Agency. The information contained in the PSMF shall be concise, accurate, and
reflect the current pharmacovigilance system in place. The format used shall allow the PSMF to
be readily updated and revised, as necessary, to incorporate operational experience, scientific and
technical developments, and changes in regulatory requirements.



2.21 Format and layout


2.21.1 The pharmacovigilance system master file may be in electronic form on condition that a
clearly arranged printed copy can be made available to the Agency. In any format, the
pharmacovigilance system master file shall be legible, complete, provided in a manner that ensures
all documentation is accessible and allows full traceability of changes.

  2.21.2       Therefore, it may be appropriate to restrict access to the pharmacovigilance system
master file to ensure appropriate control over the content and to assign specific responsibilities for
the management of the pharmacovigilance system master file in terms of change control and
archiving.

2.21.3 The pharmacovigilance system master file shall be indexed in a manner consistent to allow
easy navigation to the contents. In general, embedded documents are discouraged. The use of
electronic book-marking and searchable text is recommended. Documents such as copies of signed
statements or agreements shall be included as appendices and described in the index.

2.21.4 The Pharmacovigilance System Master File (PSMF) shall be indexed to allow easy
navigation of its contents. Embedded documents are generally discouraged. The use of electronic
bookmarking and searchable text is recommended. Copies of signed statements, agreements, and
similar documents shall be included as appendices and clearly referenced in the index.
