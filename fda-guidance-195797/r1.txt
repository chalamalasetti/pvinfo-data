14           Introduction
15   The Food and Drug Administration (FDA or Agency) is issuing this draft guidance document for
16   sponsors of Investigational Device Exemption (IDE) applications1 to the Center for Devices and
17   Radiological Health (CDRH) and Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER). It
18   introduces those sponsors to the current publicly available resources and associated content
19   developed to support the electronic submissions of IDE applications not otherwise exempted in
20   Section VI.A2 (hereafter “IDE electronic submissions”) to FDA. This draft guidance is intended
21   to represent one of several steps in meeting FDA’s commitment to the development of electronic
22   submission templates to serve as guided submission preparation tools for sponsors to improve
23   submission consistency and enhance efficiency in the review process.3 When finalized, this draft
24   guidance will also facilitate the implementation of the FDA’s mandate under section 745A(b) of
25   the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FD&C Act), amended by section 207 of the FDA
26   Reauthorization Act of 2017 (FDARA) (Pub. L. 115-52) to provide further standards for the
27   submission by electronic format, a timetable for establishment of these further standards, and
28   criteria for waivers of and exemptions from the requirements.

 74           Background
 75   Section 745A(b) of the FD&C Act, amended by section 207 of FDARA, requires that pre-
 76   submissions and submissions for devices under sections 510(k), 513(f)(2)(A), 515(c), 515(d),
 77   515(f), 520(g), 520(m), or 564 of the FD&C Act or section 351 of the Public Health Service Act,
 78   and any supplements to such pre-submissions or submissions, including appeals of those
 79   submissions, be submitted in electronic format specified by FDA beginning on such date as
 80   specified by FDA in final guidance. It also mandates that FDA issue draft guidance not later than
 81   October 1, 2019, and a final guidance not later than 1 year after the close of the public comment
 82   period, providing for further standards for the submission by electronic format, a timetable for
 83   establishment of these further standards, and criteria for waivers of and exemptions from the
 84   requirements.4
 85
 86   In addition, in the Medical Device User Fee Amendments of 2017 (MDUFA IV) Commitment
 87   Letter5 from the Secretary of Health and Human Services to Congress, FDA committed to
 88   developing “electronic submission templates that will serve as guided submission preparation
 89   tools for industry to improve submission consistency and enhance efficiency in the review
 90   process” and “[by] FY [fiscal year] 2020, the Agency will issue a draft guidance document on
 91   the use of the electronic submission templates.” In addition, the MDUFA IV Commitment Letter
 92   states that “[n]o later than 12 months after the close of the public comment period, the Agency
 93   will issue a final guidance.” The 745A(b) device parent guidance was intended to satisfy the final
 94   guidance documents referenced in section 745A(b)(3) of the FD&C Act and the MDUFA IV
 95   Commitment Letter. The Medical Device User Fee Amendments of 2022 (MDUFA V)
 96   Commitment Letter affirmed FDA’s commitment to the continued development of electronic
 97   submission templates for a variety of premarket submission types.6
 98
 99   In February 2020, CDRH piloted the use of the electronic Submission Template And Resource
100   (eSTAR) electronic submission template by launching the eSTAR Pilot Program.7 Since that
101   time, the development and availability of eSTAR templates, and new versions of those templates,
102   has expanded and currently available templates and versions can be found on FDA’s website.8
103
104   During the transition time, up to the point when IDE electronic submissions will be required (see
105   Section VI.B below), anyone can voluntarily use eSTAR for submission of IDE applications.9 As

109            Scope
110   This draft guidance describes the technical standards associated with the preparation of the
111   electronic submission template for IDE applications, that enable submission of the electronic IDE
112   solely in electronic format. The electronic submission template includes the information and
113   guided prompts FDA believes will best facilitate the collection and assembly of the elements of
114   an IDE application required under 21 CFR Part 812. This draft guidance is not intended to
115   specify the user-interface and detailed content of the eSTAR but instead is limited to establishing
116   the IDE electronic format and standards for complying with section 745A(b)(3) of the FD&C
117   Act. FDA intends to implement new versions of eSTAR as relevant policies change. FDA also
118   has an ongoing process to collect and consider public comments and stakeholder feedback,
119   which is described on FDA’s website.10
120

121            Significant Terminology
122   For the purpose of this document, the following significant terminology is described:
123
124   eCopy: An electronic copy is a duplicate device submission in electronic format of the
125   previously required paper copy submission sent to FDA.11 An electronic copy is not considered
126   to be an electronic submission, as defined below.
127
128   Electronic Submission (eSubmission): The submission package produced by an electronic
129   submission template12 that contains the data of a ‘complete’13 submission.
130
131   Electronic submission template: A guided submission preparation tool for sponsors. An
132   electronic submission template walks the sponsor through the relevant contents and components
133   for the respective premarket submission type and device to facilitate submission preparation and
134   enhance consistency, quality, and efficiency in the premarket review process.14
135   eSTAR (electronic Submission Template And Resource): An electronic submission
136   template15 built within a structured dynamic PDF that guides a user through construction of an
137   eSubmission. eSTAR is the only type of electronic submission template that is currently
138   available to facilitate the preparation of IDEs as eSubmissions. For simplicity, the electronic
139   submission created with this electronic submission template is often referred to as an eSTAR.

146       Current Electronic Submission Template Structure,
147   Format, and Use
148   The electronic submission template, eSTAR, is the only currently available electronic
149   submission template at this time to facilitate the preparation of IDE electronic submissions.
150   eSTAR consists of a collection of questions, text, logic, and prompts within a template that
151   guides a user through construction of an IDE application addressing all the elements required by
152   21 CFR Part 812. eSTAR is highly automated, includes integrated databases (e.g., FDA-
153   recognized voluntary consensus standards), and includes targeted questions designed to collect
154   specific data and information from the sponsor. eSTAR also includes applicable links to
155   regulations, relevant guidances, and other resources for the sponsor’s reference. Finally, eSTAR
156   is structured to collect and assemble content in the IDE application as an electronic submission
157   that closely follows the content of the Submission Memo And Review Template or “SMART”
158   IDE review memo template, an internal review tool used by FDA reviewers.
159
160   FDA intends16 to review and reach a decision on every IDE electronic submission within 30 days
161   from the date of receipt17 of the IDE application. For more information on FDA’s processes
162   regarding IDE application decisions, please see the guidance “FDA Decisions for Investigational
163   Device Exemption Clinical Investigations.”

173       Electronic Submission Template Waivers, Exemptions,
174   and Timing
175   Upon finalization of this draft guidance, all IDE applications, unless exempt as described in
176   Section VI.A of this draft guidance, will be required to be submitted as electronic submissions as
177   of the implementation date. An IDE Application that is not provided as an electronic submission
178   as of that date and as described in Section V above, will not be considered received unless an
179   exemption from the electronic submission requirements applies.

207   IDE electronic submission requirements and does not intend to grant requests for waiver. Given
208   the widespread availability of software to enable use of the current IDE eSTAR PDF (available
209   to download on FDA’s website), all sponsors should have the ability to provide an IDE
210   eSTAR.29
