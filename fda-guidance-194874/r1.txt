17   applicants intending to submit Premarket Approval applications (PMAs) and certain PMA
18   supplements to the Center for Devices and Radiological Health (CDRH) and Center for

78    II. Background
 79   Section 745A(b) of the FD&C Act, amended by section 207 of FDARA, requires that pre-
 80   submissions and submissions for devices under section 510(k), 513(f)(2)(A), 515(c), 515(d),
 81   515(f), 520(g), 520(m), or 564 of the FD&C Act or section 351 of the Public Health Service Act,
 82   and any supplements to such pre-submissions or submissions, including appeals of those
 83   submissions, be submitted in electronic format specified by FDA beginning on such date as
 84   specified by FDA in final guidance. It also mandates that FDA issue draft guidance not later than
 85   October 1, 2019, and a final guidance not later than 1 year after the close of the public comment
 86   period, providing for further standards for the submission by electronic format, a timetable for
 87   establishment of these further standards, and criteria for waivers of and exemptions from the
 88   requirements. 3
 89
 90   In addition, in the Medical Device User Fee Amendments of 2017 (MDUFA IV) Commitment
 91   Letter 4 from the Secretary of Health and Human Services to Congress, FDA committed to
 92   developing “electronic submission templates that will serve as guided submission preparation
 93   tools for industry to improve submission consistency and enhance efficiency in the review
 94   process” and “[by] FY [fiscal year] 2020, the Agency will issue a draft guidance document on
 95   the use of the electronic submission templates.” In addition, the MDUFA IV Commitment Letter
 96   states that “[n]o later than 12 months after the close of the public comment period, the Agency
 97   will issue a final guidance.” The 745A(b) device parent guidance was intended to satisfy the final
 98   guidance document referenced in section 745A(b)(3) of the FD&C Act and the MDUFA IV
 99   Commitment Letter. The Medical Device User Fee Amendments of 2022 (MDUFA V)
100   Commitment Letter affirmed FDA’s commitment to the continued development of electronic
101   submission templates for a variety of premarket submission types. 5
102
103   In February 2020, CDRH piloted the use of the electronic Submission Template And Resource
104   (eSTAR) electronic submission template by launching the eSTAR Pilot Program. 6 Since that
105   time, the development and availability of eSTAR templates, and new versions of those templates,
106   has expanded and currently available templates and versions can be found on FDA’s website. 7

112   III. Scope
113   This guidance describes the technical standards associated with preparation of the electronic
114   submission template for PMAs/PMA supplements that enable submission of the Designated
115   Application Types solely in electronic format. This does not include PMA Modules and Modular
116   Shells. This guidance discusses the information and guided prompts in the electronic submission
117   template that FDA believes will best facilitate the collection and assembly of the necessary
118   elements of a ‘complete’ submission.9 This guidance does not address the information needed for
119   the filing review. This guidance document will be updated accordingly as other PMA submission
120   types are implemented within the eSTAR program. This guidance is not intended to specify the
121   user-interface and detailed content of the eSTAR, but instead is limited to establishing the PMA
122   electronic format and standards for complying with section 745A(b)(3) of the FD&C Act. FDA
123   intends to implement new versions of eSTAR as relevant policies change. FDA also has an
124   ongoing process to collect and consider public comments and stakeholder feedback, which is
125   described on FDA’s website. 10

155   V. Current Electronic Submission Template Structure,
156   Format, and Use
157   The electronic submission template, eSTAR, is the only currently available electronic
158   submission template at this time to facilitate the preparation of PMA/PMA supplement electronic
159   submissions. eSTAR consists of a collection of questions, text, logic, and prompts within a
160   template that guides a user through construction of a ‘complete’ submission. 17 eSTAR is highly
161   automated, includes integrated databases (e.g., FDA product codes, FDA-recognized voluntary
162   consensus standards), and includes targeted questions designed to collect specific data and
163   information from the applicant. eSTAR also includes applicable links to regulations, relevant
164   guidances, and other resources for the applicant’s reference. Finally, eSTAR is structured to
165   collect and assemble content in the PMA/PMA supplement as an electronic submission that
166   closely follows the content of the Submission Memo And Review Template (“SMART”), a
167   review template and internal review tool used by FDA reviewers for certain PMA submission
168   types.
169
170   Given that an electronic submission properly prepared with an electronic submission template
171   should represent an administratively complete submission,18 submissions currently subject to the
172   refuse to accept (RTA) process (i.e., Original PMAs and Panel-Track Supplements) are not
173   anticipated to undergo the RTA process described in the guidance “Acceptance and Filing
174   Reviews for Premarket Approval Applications (PMAs).” However, FDA intends to employ a
175   virus scanning and technical screening process for eSTAR submissions. A technical screening
176   process is a process for verifying that eSTAR responses accurately describe the device(s) (e.g.,

195   notify the applicant electronically and the submission proceeds to substantive review.
