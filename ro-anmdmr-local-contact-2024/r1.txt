National Agency for Medicines and Medical Devices of Romania (NAMMDR)
may request the appointment of a contact person for pharmacovigilance issues at
national level, who will report to the qualified person responsible for
pharmacovigilance (EU QPPV), as per the current legislation [Romanian Law
95/2006, art 830 (5)].

Currently, the nomination by the MAH of a local contact person responsible for
pharmacovigilance in Romania is not mandatory.

To facilitate communication in the pharmacovigilance area, the MAH is
reccommended to nominate such a local contact person. It is also recommended
that the contact person resides in Romania and is medically qualified or has access
to a medically qualified person. It is necessary for the local contact person to be
trained in Pharmacovigilance by the MAH.

In case a person is nominated, the details of the local contact person for
Pharmacovigilance in Romania should be sent to the NAMMDR, through an
official address, in written or electronic format (email addresses:
registratura@anm.ro and farmacovigilenta@anm.ro), to the attention of the
Pharmacovigilance and Risk Management Unit.

The Pharmacovigilance System Master File is managed by the EU QPPV who
resides and operates in the European Union and who is responsible for the
establishment and maintenance of the pharmacovigilance system.
