About Fiji MRA

Fiji MRA is part of the Fiji Pharmaceutical & Biomedical Services Centre. It regulates medicinal products in Fiji, including medicines and medical devices, with a focus on safety, effectiveness and quality.

What Fiji MRA does
  • Registers medicines and manages prequalification.
  • Issues permits for controlled substances and import/export clearances for travellers.
  • Inspects premises that manufacture, store, distribute or sell medicines.
  • Processes licences for importers, wholesalers and other medicine licence holders, and registers and licences pharmacists.
  • Monitors medicine safety and conducts drug testing.
  • Provides secretariat support to the Fiji Medicinal Products Board and the Fiji Pharmacy Profession Board.

Australia supports the authority through the Pacific Medicines Testing Program and the Indo-Pacific Regulatory Strengthening Program. Technical assistance covers testing, market authorisation, Good Manufacturing Practice certification, import permits and pharmacovigilance.

Application guidance

Provisional medicinal product registration

Under the Medicinal Products Act 2011, medicinal products must be registered for import, manufacture, export, sale or distribution in Fiji. The Fiji Medicinal Products Board has assigned Fiji MRA to manage provisional registration.

Use the portal to submit an application. After creating an account, enter your company details in the sponsor registration form to access the provisional listing application. Have your company registration certificate, company and licensing details, and product information ready. The portal also supports streamlined registration for products already approved in Australia or New Zealand.

Supplying medicinal products to government

Manufacturers and products must meet Ministry prequalification requirements before supply. Suppliers apply through the portal for prequalification of manufacturers and finished pharmaceutical products.

  • Existing provisional register information can be transferred to a prequalification application.
  • Products approved through prequalification are added to the provisional register.
  • Check the guidelines and use the Help menu in the portal for further assistance.

Import permits

Fiji MRA assesses applications against quality, safety and efficacy standards and checks that products come from reputable manufacturers through legitimate supply chains. The Medicinal Products Act 2011 sets standards for medicinal products imported by authorised and licensed persons.

The guideline covers imports for trade, donation, personal use and travel. The portal also handles import permit applications for controlled chemicals and poisons.

Donations of medicinal products

The Ministry assesses donations against Fiji’s essential medicines policy, treatment guidelines, training needs and health system. For the requirements, see section 5.2.1 of the National Medicinal Products Policy. The Ministry also notes that financial contributions are often more helpful.

Obtain a Fiji MRA import permit before donated products enter Fiji. Use the checklist with your application.

About pharmacovigilance

Fiji MRA collects, investigates and monitors reports, provides recommendations and feedback, and shares relevant findings with international regulatory authorities, including WHO. It also issues alerts about substandard, falsified or contaminated products and defective equipment, and alerts the public to recalls where risks outweigh benefits.
