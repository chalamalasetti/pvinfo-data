15   I.         INTRODUCTION
16
17   This guidance is intended to provide recommendations to sponsors regarding eligibility criteria,
18   trial design considerations, and efficacy endpoints to enhance clinical trial data quality and to
19   foster greater efficiency in development programs for drugs for the treatment of neovascular
20   age-related macular degeneration. 2, 3
21
22   In general, FDA’s guidance documents do not establish legally enforceable responsibilities.
23   Instead, guidances describe the Agency’s current thinking on a topic and should be viewed only
24   as recommendations, unless specific regulatory or statutory requirements are cited. The use of
25   the word should in Agency guidances means that something is suggested or recommended, but
26   not required.

29   II.       CONSIDERATIONS FOR CLINICAL TRIALS 4
30
31             A.      Trial Design
32
33   Sponsors developing drugs for the treatment of neovascular age-related macular degeneration
34   should consider the following regarding trial design:
35
36         •   FDA recommends parallel-group, randomized by patient, double-masked trials in which
37             the investigational drug group demonstrates superiority over the control group.
38
39         •   Alternatively, FDA recommends parallel-group, randomized by patient, double-masked
40             trials in which the investigational drug group demonstrates noninferiority either to
41             ranibizumab injection administered intravitreally every 4 weeks or to aflibercept
42             administered intravitreally either every 4 weeks or every 8 weeks (after 3 monthly
43             injections).
44
45             B.      Comparator
46
47   Sponsors developing drugs for the treatment of neovascular age-related macular degeneration
48   should consider the following regarding comparative clinical trials:
49
50         •   Each investigational drug arm is expected to have at least one other comparative arm in
51             which the dosing frequency, criterion for dosing adjustments, and criterion for
52             interventions are the same.
53
54             C.      Trial Population
55
56   Sponsors developing drugs for the treatment of neovascular age-related macular degeneration
57   should consider the following regarding trial population:
58
59         •   Trials should include patients older than 50 years of age from relevant demographic
60             subsets, including both men and women and multiple racial/ethnic and eye color groups.
61
62         •   Patients should have choroidal neovascularization documented by fundus photography,
63             fluorescein angiography, or optical coherence tomography.
64
65         •   For a trial designed as a superiority trial, the sponsor should enroll patients with
66             neovascularization caused by age-related macular degeneration who have had visual loss
67             or would be expected to develop visual loss.

 72          •   Neovascular macular degeneration secondary to causes other than aging (such as

 76              D.       Efficacy Considerations
 77
 78   Sponsors developing drugs for the treatment of neovascular age-related macular degeneration
 79   should consider the following regarding efficacy:
 80
 81          •   In general, safety and efficacy should be demonstrated in at least two adequate and
 82              well-controlled, multicenter trials utilizing different investigative sites.
 83
 84          •   One eye per patient should be prespecified as the study eye for the purposes of the efficacy
 85              analysis even if both eyes are treated. Both eyes should be followed for safety.
 86
 87          •   FDA recommends that the sponsor demonstrate one of the following:
 88
 89               A statistically significant smaller percentage of patients with a doubling of the visual
 90                angle 5 in best corrected distance visual acuity 6 at 9 months 7 or later after the start of
 91                drug administration in the investigational drug treatment group compared to the
 92                control 8 group;
 93
 94               A statistically significant larger percentage of patients with a halving of the visual
 95                angle 9 in best corrected distance visual acuity6 at 9 months7 or later after the start of
 96                drug administration in the investigational drug treatment group compared to the
 97                control group;
 98
 99               A statistically significant difference between groups in mean best corrected distance
100                visual acuity6 of 15 or more letters at 9 months7 or later after the start of drug
101                administration.
102

114            E.      Safety Considerations
115
116   Sponsors developing drugs for the treatment of neovascular age-related macular degeneration
117   should consider the following regarding safety:
118
119        •   FDA recommends that approximately 400 or more patients using the investigational drug
120            complete treatment with a concentration of the investigational drug at least as high as
121            proposed for marketing and with a dosing frequency at least as frequent as proposed for
122            marketing.
123
124        •   Before submission of a marketing application, the sponsor should ensure that at least 300
125            patients have completed at least 9 months of follow-up after the initiation of treatment.
126
127        •   FDA recommends that at least one concurrently controlled safety trial be conducted for at
128            least 2 years’ duration. 10
129
130            F.      Clinical Evaluations
131
132   Sponsors developing drugs for the treatment of neovascular age-related macular degeneration
133   should consider the following regarding clinical evaluations:
134
135        •   At a minimum, FDA recommends sponsors perform the following evaluations in each eye
136            and report separately for each eye (regardless of which eye or eyes are treated):
137
138             Best corrected distance visual acuity 11 at every visit.
139
140             Dilated seven-field fundus photographs or equivalent wide-field views at no less than
141              6-month intervals during the first 2 years.

159            G.      Pediatrics
160
161   Neovascular age-related macular degeneration rarely occurs in the pediatric population. Sponsors
162   should consider pediatric assessment waiver requests when submitting their required pediatric
163   study plans under the Pediatric Research Equity Act. 12
