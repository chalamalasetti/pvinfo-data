TO: All Manufacturers and Potential Manufacturers of Laser Products

SUBJECT: Reproduction of Hazard Warnings in User Instructions

BACKGROUND: 21 CFR 1040.10(h)(1)(iii) requires that the user instructions
or operation manual for laser products contain legible reproductions (color
optional) and indicate locations of all labels and hazard warnings that are

required by 21 CFR 1040.10(g)

and 1040.11 to be affixed to the product.

Some manufacturers have enclosed their laser product protective housings
within additional external "cosmetic" enclosures. Such enclosures are
intended to preserve the aesthetic integrity of the products and to
discourage users from opening the products. Since protective housing
warning labels are covered by the "cosmetic" enclosures and are not visible
in operation and maintenance configurations, manufacturers have asked
