16   I.      INTRODUCTION
17
18   This guidance provides recommendations on information that should be included in the
19   prescribing information for combined hormonal contraceptives (CHCs) that contain estrogen and
20   progestin. CHCs include combined oral contraceptives (COCs), as well as non-oral products
21   such as transdermal systems and vaginal rings. Many of the labeling recommendations in this
22   guidance represent class labeling that should be included in all CHC prescribing information.
23   General advice is provided [within brackets in bold font] where modifications of the prescribing
24   information for specific products are needed.
25
26   This guidance follows the content and format requirements of the final rule “Requirements on
27   Content and Format of Labeling for Human Prescription Drug and Biological Products,” issued
28   in 2006, that amended human prescription and biological product labeling (commonly referred to
29   as the physician labeling rule (PLR)),2,3 as well as the final rule “Content and Format of Labeling
30   for Human Prescription Drug and Biological Products; Requirements for Pregnancy and

46   II.       BACKGROUND
47
48   The content and format requirements of the PLR and the PLLR (21 CFR 201.56 and 201.57)
49   required revisions to CHC labeling under certain circumstances. In addition, the labeling for
50   recently approved CHCs is consistent with the labeling requirements set forth in the PLR and the
51   PLLR. This newer CHC labeling may be different from the labeling for some older CHC
52   products. The updates to this guidance set forth labeling recommendations that represent newer
53   class labeling that should be included in all CHC prescribing information consistent with the
54   labeling requirements set forth in the PLR and PLLR (21 CFR 201.56 and 201.57). We note that
55   the specific advice provided in this guidance is not intended to cover every aspect of the PLR
56   and PLLR requirements for CHC labeling. Accordingly, we refer the reader to 21 CFR 201.56
57   and 201.57, which set forth the PLR and PLLR content and format labeling requirements. We
58   also refer the reader to the PLR Requirements for Prescribing Information web page, which lists
59   additional guidances that address specific requirements under PLR and PLLR.6

246   Arterial Events
247   CHCs increase the risk of cardiovascular events and cerebrovascular events, such as myocardial
248   infarction and stroke. The risk is greater among older women (> 35 years of age), smokers, and
249   females with hypertension, dyslipidemia, diabetes, or obesity.
250
251   [NAME] is contraindicated in women over 35 years of age who smoke [see Contraindications
252   (4)]. Cigarette smoking increases the risk of serious cardiovascular events from CHC use. This
253   risk increases with age, particularly in women over 35 years of age, and with the number of
254   cigarettes smoked.
255
256   Venous Events
257   Use of CHCs increases the risk of venous thromboembolic events (VTEs), such as deep vein
258   thrombosis and pulmonary embolism. Risk factors for VTEs include smoking, obesity, and family
