I.      Purpose

        This Standard Operating Policy and Procedure (SOPP) serves as a guide
        for Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) staff to describe
        the policies and procedures for resolving procedural and/or scientific
        disputes that cannot be resolved at the Divisional level between sponsors
        and CBER review staff regarding submissions under CBER review.

II.     Scope

        A. This SOPP applies to disputes that arise from disagreements between
           sponsors and CBER review staff regarding a regulatory submission for
           a user fee product under any of the available regulatory mechanisms
           (21 CFR 10.75, 312.48(c), 314.103(c)).

        B. This SOPP does not cover:

             1. Internal disputes that arise from scientific disagreements between
                Food and Drug Administration (FDA) review staff regarding the
                review of sponsor submissions (refer to SOPP 8006: Resolution of
                Differences in Scientific Judgment in the Review Process).

           2. Sponsor requests for CBER Ombudsman assistance in facilitating
              informal resolution of a specific issue, including, but not limited to, a
              procedural or administrative matter regarding a product.

           3. Available mechanisms beyond 10.75(a)(3) by which individuals
              outside of FDA who disagree with a decision or action taken by
              CBER submit requests for hearings under 21 CFRs parts 12-16 or
              Citizen Petitions under 21 CFR 10.30.

           4. Procedures related to other individuals or entities that wish to
              request review of a scientific and/or medical issue regarding an
              application regulated by CBER: 21 CFR 10.75(c) may provide a
              separate mechanism for non-sponsors to request internal Agency
              review of a CBER decision on such issues.

III.   Background

       A. Regulations (21 CFR 10.75) provide a mechanism for any sponsor,
          applicant, or manufacturer of a drug, device, or biological product to
          obtain formal review of an FDA decision by raising the matter with the
          supervisor of the employee who made the decision. If the issue is not
          resolved at the primary management (i.e., division) level, the interested
          person may request that the matter be reviewed at the next higher
          management level. This process may continue through the FDA’s
          chain of command (i.e., through the Centers to the FDA Commissioner
          of Food and Drugs).

       B. Regulations for dispute resolution during the investigational new drug
          application (IND) process (21 CFR 312.48) and the new drug
          application (NDA)/abbreviated new drug application (ANDA) process
          (21 CFR 314.103) specifically establish procedures for the resolution of
          scientific and procedural matters at the division level and subsequent
          formal review of decisions through center management.

       C. Actions that may be undertaken within the Center as part of the FDR
          process include, but are not limited to, requesting clarifying
          information, scheduling a meeting with the sponsor, presenting the
          issue(s) to an advisory committee or issuing a decision to grant or
          deny the appeal.

IV.    Definitions

       A. Appeal – For the purposes of this SOPP, an “appeal” is a formal
          sponsor request for FDR.

       B. CBER Ombudsman – The individual in the Office of the Center
          Director (OCD) who investigates complaints and assists in formal
          dispute resolution.

       C. Clarifying Information – Information submitted in support of a Formal
          Dispute Resolution Request that elaborates on or explains data and
          analyses already reviewed by the division.

       D. Deciding Official – The individual responsible for reviewing the
          relevant files and providing a recommendation on the matter(s) at
          issue in a Formal Dispute Resolution Request (FDRR). The Deciding
          Official may hold one of the following positions: Deputy Office Director,
          Office Director, Associate CBER Director, Deputy CBER Director, or
          CBER Director.

       E. External Expertise – Additional expertise that may be sought to
          provide input into the matter at issue in the FDRR, such as the
          Network of Experts, Special Government Employees (SGE), or
          Advisory Committee (Panel) Members.

       F. Formal Dispute Resolution (FDR) – The process undertaken to
          address scientific and/or medical disputes between a sponsor and
          CBER as related to the sponsor’s application for a user fee product.

       G. Formal Dispute Resolution Request (FDRR) – Formal sponsor
          request for appeal of a scientific and/or medical matter related to an
          application for a user fee product under any of the following available
          regulatory mechanisms: 21 CFR 10.75, 312.48(c), or 314.103(c).

       H. Sponsor – For the purposes of this SOPP, the party, e.g. submitter,
          applicant, manufacturer, who submits a pre-submission,
          investigational, marketing, or post-marketing submission to CBER.
          NOTE: The sponsor makes the decision to request formal dispute
          resolution in accordance with the Guidance for Industry and Review
          Staff: Formal Dispute Resolution: Sponsor Appeals Above the Division
          Level after CBER action or inaction.

V.     Policy

       A. CBER is committed to resolving scientific and procedural differences
          with sponsors as quickly and amicably as possible through the
          cooperative exchange of information. Disagreements with sponsors will
          be addressed at successively higher levels of the chain of command at
          the request of the sponsor or until resolution is achieved.

       B. Issue(s) that cannot be resolved at one level can be appealed, in
          writing, to the next level in the chain of command (with a copy to the

                                                                        Page 3 of 12
Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research                              SOPP 8005


           appropriate individual at the prior level). All formal dispute resolution
           requests (FDRR) will be coordinated by the CBER Ombudsman, so
           that they can be triaged, tracked and included in the necessary reports.

       C. CBER encourages sponsors to contact the CBER Ombudsman before
          submitting a request for FDR. The CBER Ombudsman handles both
          informal requests and FDRRs, so if the sponsor intends to submit an
          FDRR, they should be clear in its submission that the request is an
          FDRR.

       D. In most cases, the sponsor will be notified of the decision within 30
          days from the date of receipt of the FDRR in CBER or within 30 days
          of the Advisory Committee meeting, if the matter was presented to
          one. Initial notification may be made through rapid means of
          communication, such as by telephone or email. Written confirmation of
          the decision will be provided to the sponsor within 14 days of the initial
          rapid communication.

       E. For products covered by PDUFA, GDUFA, and BsUFA, the procedures
          and timeframes set forth in this document will be used when the
          requirements for FDRR are met.

       F. For FDRRs involving applications not subject to PDUFA, or those
          related to applications or submissions for CBER-regulated medical
          devices covered under the Medical Device User Fee Act (MDUFA), the
          timelines described in this SOPP will be met as resources permit.

       G. If a meeting or teleconference must take place to discuss the formal
          review of any FDA decisions or actions taken on a CBER medical
          device submission, the meetings will be scheduled as a Submission
          Issue Meeting as described in the Guidance for Industry and Food and
          Drug Administration Staff: Requests for Feedback and Meetings for
          Medical Device Submissions: The Q-Submission Program. For human
          drug or biological applications (i.e., BLAs and NDAs) covered by
          PDUFA a Type A meeting will be scheduled as described in Guidance
          for Industry: Formal Meetings Between the FDA and Sponsors or
          Applicants of PDUFA Products.

       F. After any type of response letter regarding the FDRR has been issued
          by CBER, the review clock is stopped. Upon official receipt of a
          complete response submitted by the sponsor, the review clock is reset
          to day zero and restarted.

       G. Clarifying information must not include new information or reanalysis of
          data that has not been reviewed by the division. The FDA considers
          new analyses of previously reviewed data to be new information

Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research                              SOPP 8005


           because the original deciding official might have made a different
           decision had they had the opportunity to review the new analysis.

       H. If the decision is to disagree with the FDRR, (i.e., Deciding Official
          agrees with the FDA staff that made the initial decision), the response
          must include the reasons for the disagreement and, where applicable,
          any additional action available to the sponsor to continue pursuit of the
          appeal.

VI.    Responsibilities

       A. CBER Ombudsman – Coordinates the routing, review, system entry,
          explains all regulatory processes related to FDR to the sponsor,
          conducts a preliminary review of the sponsor’s FDRR to evaluate
          whether the appeal satisfies the procedural criteria, issues
          correspondence, uploads all FDR documents into the Dispute
          Resolution SharePoint site, and makes all contacts with the sponsor.

       B. Deciding Official – Performs the review and signs the written
          response to the sponsor. NOTE: The Deciding Official at each level of
          appeal has the same responsibilities and follows the same procedures
          as at the previous level.

       C. CBER’s Regulatory Information Branch (RIB) – Prepares a monthly
          status report on all FDRRs for review by the Center Director, Deputy
          Center Director, Associate Center Directors, and Office Directors;
          prepares the year end and status reports for inclusion in the Report to
          Congress.

       D. Review Team – Prepares any documentation needed for briefing
          Deciding Official. Review team includes staff member(s) involved in
          issuing the decision that is being appealed.

VII.   Procedures

       A. If a sponsor notifies a CBER recipient of their consideration or intent
          to submit or pursue an FDRR: [CBER recipient]

           1. Immediately notify the CBER Ombudsman of the sponsor's intent
              by forwarding the relevant communication.

           2. Contact the sponsor via email and:

               a. Recommend consultation with the CBER Ombudsman prior
                  to submitting an FDRR to attempt informal resolution of the
                  matter.


                                                                         Page 5 of 12
Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research                              SOPP 8005


               b. Provide the CBER Ombudsman contact information, available at
                  Contact an Ombudsman at FDA.

               c. Inform the sponsor that if proceeding with an FDRR, they must
                  submit that formal dispute resolution request under the parent
                  application if the dispute pertains to an active regulatory
                  submission and a copy should be sent to the CBER
                  Ombudsman.

       B. Receive the FDRR from the Document Control Center. [CBER
          Ombudsman]

           1. For FDRRs related to medical devices, verify that the eCopy is
              reviewable, and that all issues are resolved prior to uploading. If the
              eCopy is not reviewable, place the submission on eCopy hold until
              an acceptable replacement eCopy is received and successfully
              loaded.

       C. Conduct a preliminary review of the sponsor’s FDRR to evaluate
          whether the appeal satisfies the procedural criteria for acceptance and
          may proceed through the FDR process. [CBER Ombudsman]

           1. Verify that the request relates to a decision subject to 21 CFR
              10.75.

           2. If a request is found to have been inappropriately submitted to
              CBER, redirect the request to the appropriate Center within the
              FDA.

           3. Check the FDRR submission against Section IV of the Guidance for
              Industry and Review Staff: Formal Dispute Resolution: Sponsor
              Appeals Above the Division Level Guidance for Industry and
              Review Staff to confirm that all submission requirements have been
              met.

               a. If the FDRR satisfies the procedural criteria described in Section
                  IV of the Guidance, accept the FDRR and start the 30-day
                  review clock from the date of CBER’s receipt.

                  i.   Issue a letter or email to the sponsor acknowledging receipt
                       and acceptance of the FDRR. The communication should
                       include the following:

                       a) The date the FDRR was received.

                       b) The name of the Deciding Official assigned to review the
                          FDRR.

Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research                              SOPP 8005




                       c) The Center's projected response date.

                       d) Sponsor-Deciding Official Meeting information as
                          applicable, with the proposed meeting date if granted.

                       e) If the sponsor requested an External Expert or Advisory
                          Committee Consultation, acknowledge receipt of the
                          request and advise the sponsor that CBER is considering
                          it and will provide further guidance in a timely manner.

               b. If the FDRR is missing any required elements that can be
                  promptly obtained, contact the sponsor to request the missing
                  information.

                    i. Upon receipt of the requested information, conduct a
                       preliminary review to confirm administrative completeness
                       and initiate the 30-day review clock from the date CBER
                       receives the requested information.

               c. If the FDRR does not satisfy the procedural criteria described in
                  Section IV of the Guidance or if the matter is not appropriate for
                  an FDRR (see Section III.B. of the Guidance for examples (i.e.,
                  the FDRR will not be accepted), send a letter to the sponsor
                  specifying the reasons why the request was not accepted.

       D. Determine the appropriate Deciding Official who will review and
          respond to the FDRR, usually the highest management level in the
          reviewing office, or if the office director has already had substantial
          involvement in the decision being appealed by the sponsor, the next
          level of management in CBER OCD, or their designee. [CBER
          Ombudsman]

       E. Upload the complete FDRR to the Dispute Resolution SharePoint site
          and email the folder link to the Deciding Official, including CBER
          receipt date and target decision date. [CBER Ombudsman]

       F. Confirm receipt of the FDRR by emailing a statement to the CBER
          Ombudsman indicating that the FDRR has received and whether all
          information in the FDRR is understood or requires clarification.
          [Deciding Official]

       G. Review the FDRR to determine whether all information is understood
          or if additional clarifying information is needed to render a decision
          (i.e., interim response). [Deciding Official]

           1. If additional clarifying information is needed:

               a. Within 30 days of CBER's receipt of the FDRR, discuss with
                  the sponsor any additional clarifying information needed and
                  provide an explanation for why an interim response is being
                  issued instead of a final decision on the appeal.

                   NOTE: The review clock stops upon issuance of the interim
                   response.

               b. Within 14 calendar days of the telephone discussion, send
                  written confirmation of the requested clarifying information to the
                  sponsor.

                   NOTE: Clarifying information does not include new information
                   or reanalysis of data that has not been reviewed by the division.
                   The FDA considers new analyses of previously reviewed data to
                   be new information because the original deciding official might
                   have made a different decision had he or she had the
                   opportunity to review the new analysis.

               c. Upon receipt of the requested clarifying information by the
                  Deciding Official, the review clock starts, with a goal of
                  rendering a decision within 30 calendar days from the date of
                  receipt.

           2. If no additional clarifying information is needed:

               a. Within 30 days of CBER's receipt of the FDRR or of the meeting
                  date, if a meeting is held, issue a final decision on the appeal to
                  the sponsor.

       H. If a consultative review by other segments of the Agency (e.g., Office
          of Chief Counsel) is needed, arrange such review as appropriate:
          [Deciding Official/CBER Ombudsman]

           1. Call the appropriate segment of the Agency to arrange consultative
              review.

           2. Send the FDRR to the appropriate official together with a list of the
              specific questions or issues to be addressed and the due date for
              receipt of comments.

           3. Include consultative review comments in the documentation for the
              FDRR.

       I. If a meeting with the sponsor is required to address the issue(s):
          [Deciding Official]

           1. For biological products, schedule it as a Type A Meeting. Refer to
              SOPP 8101.1: Regulatory Meetings with Sponsors and Applicants
              for Drugs and Biological Products.

           2. For medical device products, schedule it as a Submission Issue
              Meeting. Refer to Guidance for Industry and Food and Drug
              Administration Staff: Requests for Feedback and Meetings for
              Medical Device Submissions: The Q-Submission Program for
              additional information.

               Note: For meetings associated with FDRRs, the 30-calendar day
               FDRR decision timeline begins following the date of the meeting.

       J. Decide whether any request for FDA Advisory Committee review in the
          FDRR is appropriate for consideration by an FDA Advisory Committee:
          [Deciding Official]

           1. If granted:

               a. Arrange for the appropriate FDA Advisory Committee to
                  consider the issue. [Deciding Official]

               b. Within 30 days of the Center's receipt of the complete FDRR,
                  notify the sponsor that the issue has been placed on the
                  Advisory Committee agenda. NOTE: The meeting date will be
                  determined based on Advisory Committee availability. [CBER
                  Ombuds]

               c. Present the issue to the Advisory Committee. [Deciding
                  Official]

               d. Within 30 calendar days of the presentation at the Advisory
                  Committee meeting, determine whether to grant or deny the
                  FDRR and notify the sponsor of the decision. [Deciding
                  Official]

           2. If denied:

               a. Draft a letter to the sponsor explaining why the request for
                  Advisory Committee review was not granted and send the draft
                  to CBER Ombudsman for review. [Deciding Official]

Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research                              SOPP 8005


               b. Provide the signed response letter to the CBER Ombudsman.
                  [Deciding Official]

               c. Issue the signed letter to the sponsor. [CBER Ombudsman]

        K. FDRR Decision (i.e., grant or deny the FDRR)

           1. Review the FDRR and decide whether to grant or deny the request.
              [Deciding Official]

           2. Within 30 calendar days of CBER receipt of the original FDRR or
              within 30 calendar days of CBER receipt of any requested clarifying
              information, issue decision letter to the sponsor. [CBER
              Ombudsman]

           3. Notify the Deciding Official, Division Directors, Office Director,
              Deputy Center Directors or Center Director, and others that have
              been involved (as deemed appropriate) regarding the decision
              conveyed to the sponsor. [CBER Ombudsman]

           4. Compile all materials related to the FDRR and send them to the
              CBER Ombudsman. [Deciding Official]

           5. Upon receipt of all documentation from the Deciding Official, upload
              the final documentation into the Dispute Resolution SharePoint site
              and send it to RIB to upload it into administrative file and close out
              the FDRR. [CBER Ombuds]

        L. FDRR Reporting

           1. Provide an update every 6 months on all active PDUFA related
              FDRs to the CBER RIB. [CBER Ombuds]

           2. Prepare year-end summary reports and status reports on all
              PDUFA related FDRs for inclusion in the Report to Congress. The
              reports should include statistics on FDRs accepted, resolved,
              granted, and denied, as well as average resolution timeframes.
              [RIB in consultation with CBER Ombudsman]
