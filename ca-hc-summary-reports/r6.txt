 Updated guidance for summary reporting requirements

 The current version of Preparing and Submitting Summary Reports for Marketed Drugs and Natural Health Products - Guidance Document for Industry was published on May 23, 2018.

 An updated guidance for summary reporting will come into effect on October 1, 2026.

 Revised guidance on preparing and submitting summary reports for marketed drugs and natural health products 

 The updates clarify long-standing requirements to prepare annual summary reports and make clear that annual summary reports should only be provided to the Minister upon receipt of a written request from Health Canada.

 The updates also describe how to notify the Minister of significant changes to what is known about the product's risks and benefits, using a new form. These updates also explain that you should notify Health Canada of a significant change only if it meets the threshold outlined in guidance, per the definition.

 As part of Health Canada's red tape reduction efforts, these changes are intended to reduce burden by minimizing duplicate reporting and over-reporting.

 These changes reinforce Health Canada's support for a lifecycle approach to the regulation of drugs and natural health products, within the current regulatory frameworks.

 Consultations

 The proposed updates were published in October 2025 for 60 days to invite comments from stakeholders.

 We received a total of 132 comments from industry and other stakeholders. The key themes of these comments were:

 Clarity around criteria and formatting for Canadian-specific information

 The desirability of international alignment

 Support for a risk-based approach

 The need for clarity regarding

 format

 required content

 timing of notification

 We considered all comments when updating the guidance document and the relevant internal processes.

 Implementation of the updated guidance

 The updated guidance will take effect as of October 1, 2026.

 Although we will not be holding additional formal consultations on this guidance, we may consider feedback when we review the guidance and regulations for future updates. You may submit your feedback by email to mhpdpolicy-politiquesdpsc@hc-sc.gc.ca .
