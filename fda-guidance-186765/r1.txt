This guidance represents the current thinking of the Food and Drug Administration (FDA or
Agency) on this topic. It does not establish any rights for any person and is not binding on
FDA or the public. You can use an alternative approach if it satisfies the requirements of the
applicable statutes and regulations. To discuss an alternative approach, contact the FDA staff
or Office responsible for this guidance as listed on the title page.


I.      Introduction
This guidance provides information on the most frequently asked questions regarding the
transfer or sale of a 510(k) clearance from one 510(k) holder to another. Under section 510(k)
of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FD&C Act) and its implementing regulations,
any person required to register under section 510 / 21 CFR 807.20 must generally submit a
premarket notification submission, or “510(k),” if the device is being introduced into
commercial distribution for the first time, or the device is one that the person currently has in
commercial distribution or is reintroducing into commercial distribution, but that is about to
be significantly changed or modified in design, components, method of manufacture, or
intended use.1
This guidance discusses when the sale or transfer of a 510(k) would not require the new holder
of a 510(k) to submit a new 510(k) under applicable law, as well as circumstances under
which entities must use the FDA-assigned premarket submission number of the cleared 510(k)
when listing their device.

In general, FDA’s guidance documents do not establish legally enforceable responsibilities.
Instead, guidances describe the Agency’s current thinking on a topic and should be viewed only
as recommendations, unless specific regulatory or statutory requirements are cited. The use of
the word should in Agency guidances means that something is suggested or recommended, but

II. Scope
This guidance applies to both the previous and the new holder of a 510(k) after the 510(k)
clearance for a specific device is sold or transferred from one person to another.

III. Definitions
For purposes of this guidance, we will use the following definitions:

510(k) clearance: FDA’s order declaring the subject device to be substantially equivalent to a
legally marketed predicate device.2

510(k) holder: The singular person who possesses the 510(k) clearance for a device. The 510(k)
holder may be a manufacturer, which may be a specifications developer.

Contract manufacturer: A person who manufactures a device for or on behalf of a
specifications developer or any other person.3

Contract sterilizer: A person who sterilizes a device for or on behalf of a specifications
developer or any other person.4

Initial importer: Any importer who furthers the marketing of a device from a foreign
manufacturer to the person who makes final delivery or sale of the device to the ultimate
consumer or user, but does not repackage, or otherwise change the container, wrapper, or
labeling of the device or device package.5

Labeler: (1) Any person who causes a label to be applied to a device with the intent that
the device will be commercially distributed without any intended subsequent replacement
or modification of the label; and (2) Any person who causes the label of a device to be
replaced or modified with the intent that the device will be commercially distributed without
any subsequent replacement or modification of the label, except that the addition of the
name of, and contact information for, a person who distributes the device, without making
any other changes to the label, is not a modification for the purposes of determining
whether a person is a labeler.6

Manufacturer: A person who makes by chemical, physical, biological, or other procedures, any
article that meets the definition of “device” in section 201(h) of the FD&C Act.7

Person: Includes individuals, partnerships, corporations, and associations as defined under

Relabeler: A person who changes the content of the labeling from that supplied from the
original manufacturer for distribution under the establishment’s own name. A relabeler
does not include establishments that add their own name to but otherwise do not change
the original labeling.

Remanufacturer: A person who processes, conditions, renovates, repackages, restores, or
does any other act to a finished device that significantly changes the finished device’s
performance or safety specifications, or intended use.8

Repackager: A person who packages finished devices from bulk or repackages devices
made by a manufacturer into different containers. A repackager does not include an entity
that only places finished packaged devices into shipping containers for transportation.

Reprocessor of single-use devices: A person who performs additional processing and
manufacturing on a single-use device that was previously used on a patient for the
purpose of an additional single use on a patient.9

Specifications developer: A person who initiates specifications for a device that is
manufactured by a second party for subsequent commercial distribution by the person
initiating specifications.10


IV. Questions and Answers
    1. Who needs to register and list?

An owner or operator of an establishment who is engaged in the manufacture, preparation,
propagation, compounding, assembly, or processing of a device intended for human use is
generally required to register the establishment and submit listing information for all devices in
commercial distribution.11

     4. I obtained a 510(k) clearance from another company (via transfer, sale, etc.). Do I
        need to submit a new 510(k)?

When a 510(k) clearance for a specific device is sold or transferred from one person to another
and the device is not significantly changed or modified in design, components, method of
manufacture, or intended use,19 the new 510(k) holder does not need to submit a new 510(k),
since the new 510(k) holder would not be proposing “to begin the introduction or delivery for
introduction into interstate commerce for commercial distribution” of a device.20 See question 10
for more details about the obligations of the 510(k) holder regarding a transfer or sale of a
510(k).

     5. What are the listing requirements for a new 510(k) holder?

When a 510(k) clearance for a specific device is sold or transferred from one person to another,
if the new 510(k) holder is engaged in any of the activities requiring listing, 21 CFR 807.25
requires the new 510(k) holder to list the device in FDA’s electronic device registration and
listing system.21 The system assigns a new unique listing number when the new 510(k) holder
creates the listing.22

For further information about registering and listing, see the webpage, “How to Register and
List.”

     6. When should a new 510(k) holder register and list?

The new 510(k) holder must, if not previously entered into an operation described in 21 CFR
807.20(a), register within 30 days after entering into such an operation and submit device listing
information, including the FDA-assigned 510(k) premarket notification submission number of
the cleared device,23 at that time.24

     7. Which cleared 510(k) premarket notification submission number must the new
        510(k) holder enter in FDA’s electronic device registration and listing system?

Each device listing for a 510(k)-cleared device requires inclusion of the FDA-assigned premarket
submission number of the cleared 510(k).28

If the device is not significantly changed or modified in design, components, method of
manufacture, or intended use,29 the new 510(k) holder must enter the original FDA-assigned
premarket submission number unless submitting a new 510(k), in which case the new 510(k)
holder would supply the new FDA-assigned premarket submission number, in FDA’s electronic
device registration and listing system.30
If the new 510(k) holder has significantly changed or modified the device in design, components,
method of manufacture, or intended use, the new 510(k) holder must generally submit a new
510(k)31 and, when listing following clearance of the new 510(k), would enter the new FDA-
assigned premarket submission number in FDA’s electronic device registration and listing
system.32

     9. Who can use a 510(k) holder’s 510(k) number to list a device?

Anyone who is required to list a cleared device, including contract manufacturers, contract
sterilizers, repackagers, and relabelers, is required to use the FDA-assigned premarket
submission number of the cleared 510(k) to list the device.38 The 510(k) holder is also
responsible for complying with applicable requirements arising under the FD&C Act.

Initial importers may fulfill their listing obligation for a device for which they did not initiate or
develop the specifications for the device or repackage or relabel the device by submitting the
name and address of the manufacturer.39

     11. Who must update information about a 510(k) transfer or sale?

FDA commonly receives notifications from individuals that a 510(k) clearance has been
transferred to that individual from a previous 510(k) holder, and asking FDA to update the
information in the registration and listing databases. However, it is the responsibility of the
owner or operator of the establishment44 to ensure that it is meeting the registration and listing
requirements of the FD&C Act. FDA is not required to update registration or listing
information in response to such notifications.

     12. How should I obtain copies of 510(k) records?

An entity wishing to have a copy of any 510(k) records may ask the prior owner for a copy of
all documents, or may submit a Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) request.45 Additionally,
CDRH posts 510(k) records previously released to the public through FOIA requests in the
510(k) database.
