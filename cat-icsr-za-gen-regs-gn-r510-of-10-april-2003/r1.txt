 REQUIREMENTS FOR THERAPEUTIC EQUIVALENCE


 2.   (1) A medicine is considered therapeutically equivalentto another medicineif both medicines-


      (a) are     pharmaceutically equivalent, i.e., contain the same amount of active substances in the same
              dosage form, meet the same or comparable standards and are intended to be administered by
              the same route; and



      (b)     after administration in the samemolar dose, their effects with respect to both efficacy and safety
              are essentially thesame.
